2D Unity Game

2Die or not 2Die is a simplistic retro-style top-down puzzle shooter game I built using the Unity game engine cobined with C# scripting. It involves a simple premise where the player must either survive for as long as possible in an arena with endless waves of enemies, or complete levels of progressing difficulty while contending with puzzle-like elements.

As this was my second large project in Unity, I could focus more on high-level design and user experience. I decided to forgo complex graphics and focus on simple shapes and colours to make enemies, puzzles and other interactive elements clear & easily memorable to the player.

What I Learned

Planning

Through this project, I learned the importance of planning and design in game development - especially practices that involve continually evalutaing plans & designs. Planning this way is crucial for me in order to complete a project to a high quality as it provides a clear roadmap and scope of development. Compared to previous projects where I did not plan this way, this project's development was much more efficient and organised, allowing me to better gauge my progress.

Collaboration

I was not the only person who worked on this project, I worked with a team of Audio & Music Technology students to create the game's audio and music elements. Because of this, I learned the importance of clear two-way communication - ensuring that both myself and the students were on the same page about the themes, sylistic choices and deliverables that were being aimed for. This included feedback from both parties as to what I wanted for the game and what they could create
