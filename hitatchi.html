Hitatchi Rail UK

During a week of work experience with Hitatchi Rail UK's Train Management System (TMS) team I got to look at and understand how modern trains utilise software and data for normal operations.

I learned what several on-board databses are used for and how they interact with the TMS, then completed a practical task on a Hitatchi hardware simulation - modifiying and testing SDO (selective door opening) data to trigger different doors opening depending on location & direction of travel.

What I Learned

Attention to Detail

The biggest takeaway I had from this experience was that even the smallest changes in the system can have significant impacts - such as the changing of a door opening or not could lead to a case where a passenger steps out of a door directly onto the track which is a huge saftey hazard. This taught me the importance of following procedure wherein methodically testing is essential rather than assuming something will function how I think it will.

Professionalism

Working at a live depot with working professionals allowed me to ask questions about their field of work and observe their practices, which solidified my goal of working in that industry.
